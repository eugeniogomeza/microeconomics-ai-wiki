## TL;DR

Grant Sanderson is the creator of 3Blue1Brown, widely regarded as the best mathematics visualization channel on YouTube. He uses a custom-built animation engine (Manim) to make abstract mathematical concepts — including neural networks and transformers — visually intuitive. His 2024 series on transformers is the canonical accessible explanation of how LLMs work.

## Key Work

- **3Blue1Brown YouTube channel** — 193+ videos spanning linear algebra, calculus, probability, neural networks, and transformers.
- **Manim** — Open-source mathematical animation engine, now with its own community of contributors.
- **Neural Networks series (2020)** — Four videos building intuition for what neural networks are, how gradient descent works, and what backpropagation really does.
- **Transformers series (2024)** — Three videos explaining GPT architecture, attention mechanisms, and how transformers store facts.
