# Joshua Gans

**Type:** Person / Academic / Author  
**Title:** Professor of Strategic Management, Jeffrey S. Skoll Chair of Technical Innovation and Entrepreneurship  
**Institution:** Rotman School of Management, University of Toronto  
**Related:** [[mit-press]], [[prediction-machines]], [[microeconomics-of-ai]]

## TL;DR

Joshua Gans is a leading economist studying the strategy and economics of innovation. His 2025 open-access book *The Microeconomics of Artificial Intelligence* builds a rigorous microeconomic framework for understanding AI as a prediction technology, expanding on his earlier work *Prediction Machines* (2018) with Agrawal and Goldfarb.

## Profile

Gans specializes in the intersection of economics, technology, and strategy. His work has evolved from general innovation economics to a focused analysis of AI's microeconomic properties — demand, supply, pricing, and policy.

## Key Works

- *The Microeconomics of Artificial Intelligence* (MIT Press, 2025)
- *Prediction Machines* (2018, with Ajay Agrawal and Avi Goldfarb)
- *The Disruption Dilemma* (MIT Press)
- *Innovation + Equality* (MIT Press)
