# Andrej Karpathy

**Type:** Person  
**Known For:** Founding member of OpenAI, former Director of AI at Tesla, creator of nanoGPT and llm.c, popular deep learning educator

## TL;DR

Andrej Karpathy is a Slovak-Canadian computer scientist and one of the most visible AI researchers of the 2020s. He earned his PhD at Stanford under Fei-Fei Li, co-founded OpenAI in 2015, led Tesla's Autopilot AI team from 2017 to 2022, and briefly rejoined OpenAI in 2023 before leaving to found his own AI company. His YouTube channel and open-source projects (micrograd, nanoGPT, llm.c) are among the most influential free educational resources in deep learning.

## Profile

Karpathy's career spans the full arc of modern AI's commercial and research rise:

- **Stanford (PhD, 2011–2015)** — Research on computer vision and deep learning, including his influential "Deep Visual-Semantic Alignments" thesis.
- **OpenAI (Founding Member, 2015–2017)** — Joined as one of the first research scientists. Contributed to early GPT work and robotics before leaving for Tesla.
- **Tesla (Director of AI, 2017–2022)** — Led the neural network architecture and training infrastructure for Tesla Autopilot and Full Self-Driving (FSD). Scaled from small experiments to training on massive video datasets from Tesla's fleet.
- **OpenAI (2023–2024)** — Briefly returned as a research scientist before leaving to start his own venture.
- **Entrepreneur / Educator (2024–present)** — Building his own AI company while continuing his educational YouTube channel and open-source projects.

His educational approach is distinctive: he doesn't lecture from slides — he types every line of code live, explaining every tensor shape and gradient as he goes. This "building from scratch" philosophy has made his videos the standard reference implementation for topics like backpropagation, transformers, and GPT.
