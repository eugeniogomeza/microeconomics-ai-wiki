# Jeremy Howard

**Type:** Person  
**Known For:** Co-founder of fast.ai, creator of fastai library, deep learning educator

## TL;DR

Jeremy Howard is a prominent deep learning researcher and educator. He co-founded fast.ai with the mission of making deep learning accessible to coders without requiring a PhD. He created the fastai library, built on PyTorch, which dramatically simplifies training state-of-the-art models.

## Profile

Howard has been a key figure in democratizing deep learning. His Practical Deep Learning for Coders course has been taken by hundreds of thousands of students worldwide. The fastai library is used in research and industry for rapid prototyping and training.
