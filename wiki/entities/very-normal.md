## TL;DR

A statistics and R programming educator focused on making simulation studies and advanced statistical methods accessible. His Monte Carlo crash course is one of the most practical tutorials on designing, running, and scaling simulation studies — from a laptop to an HPC cluster.

## Profile

Christian creates content that bridges academic statistics with practical implementation. His tutorials are dense, code-forward, and assume some familiarity with R and statistical concepts. The Monte Carlo video covers:
- Hypothesis test power comparisons
- Parallel computing with `furrr`
- HPC job scheduling with SLURM
- File management and checkpointing for long simulations
