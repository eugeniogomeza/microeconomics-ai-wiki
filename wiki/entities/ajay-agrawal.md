# Ajay Agrawal

**Type:** Person / Economist / Academic  
**Institution:** Rotman School of Management, University of Toronto  
**Co-founder:** Creative Destruction Lab (CDL)  
**Related:** [[joshua-gans]], [[avi-goldfarb]], [[prediction-machines]], [[mit-microeconomics-ai]]

## TL;DR

Ajay Agrawal is a professor and economist who, with Joshua Gans and Avi Goldfarb, authored *Prediction Machines* (2018), the book that popularized the "AI = prediction" framework. He also runs the Creative Destruction Lab at Rotman, one of the world's leading startup accelerators for science and technology companies.

## Key Works

- *Prediction Machines: The Simple Economics of Artificial Intelligence* (2018, with Gans and Goldfarb)
- *Power and Prediction: The Disruptive Economics of Artificial Intelligence* (2022, with Gans and Goldfarb)
- *The Microeconomics of Artificial Intelligence* (2025, Gans solo)
