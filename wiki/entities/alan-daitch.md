# Alan Daitch

**Type:** Person  
**AKA:** @alandaitch  
**Known For:** Spanish-language AI educator, co-founder of Digodat (sold to S4 Capital), LinkedIn Top Voice LATAM 2026

## TL;DR

Alan Daitch is a leading Spanish-speaking AI and tech educator with a massive cross-platform presence (~315K Instagram, ~134.9K TikTok). Former co-founder of Digodat (Google's #1 analytics partner in LATAM, sold to S4 Capital in 2020), former VP Data LATAM, Google Top Contributor, and LinkedIn Top Voice. He produces free courses on ChatGPT, Google Gemini, and N8N automation, explicitly refusing to sell them. Hosts the podcast "El Corazón de la Máquina."

## Profile

Daitch's enterprise credibility comes from co-founding Digodat, a data analytics company that became Google's top partner in Latin America before its acquisition by S4 Capital in 2020. He held senior data leadership roles and now focuses on making AI accessible to Spanish-speaking professionals through free content.

His tagline "IA y Tech sin humo" (AI and Tech without the hype) captures his approach: practical, immediately applicable, and free of buzzword inflation.
