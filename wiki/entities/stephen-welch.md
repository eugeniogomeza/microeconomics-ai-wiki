## TL;DR

Stephen Welch is the founder of Welch Labs, a YouTube channel focused on machine learning, AI, and mathematics education. Best known for the "Neural Networks Demystified" series (2014–2015), which builds a complete neural network from scratch in Python. Currently writing "The Welch Labs Illustrated Guide to AI."

## Key Work

- **Neural Networks Demystified** — 7-part YouTube series + GitHub repo building a neural network from scratch.
- **Welch Labs channel** — Covers ML, computer vision, and technical storytelling.
- **The Welch Labs Illustrated Guide to AI** — Book in development/pre-order.
