# Josh Starmer

**Type:** Person  
**Known For:** Creator of StatQuest, statistics and machine learning educator

## TL;DR

Josh Starmer is a biostatistician and the creator of StatQuest — one of the most popular statistics and machine learning education channels on YouTube. His signature style uses hand-drawn whiteboard animations, gentle humor, and step-by-step derivations to make intimidating topics accessible. He holds a PhD from the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill and worked as a research scientist before committing full-time to StatQuest.

## Profile

Starmer started StatQuest in 2011 while working as a research scientist. The channel grew from answering questions for his lab colleagues to reaching millions of learners worldwide. His approach — "explain it simply, then show the math" — fills a critical gap in ML education: many courses show code or theory, but few walk through the *steps* as patiently as StatQuest.

His content spans classical statistics (regression, hypothesis testing, ANOVA) through modern ML (neural networks, random forests, boosting, deep learning) and into bioinformatics applications. The PCA series alone has over 10 million views and is widely cited as the clearest introduction to the topic.
