# Domingo Gomes

**Type:** Person  
**AKA:** NewEsc, @newesc, @New3sc  
**Known For:** Founder of NewEsc (tech media brand), TikTok tech creator (~1.3M followers)

## TL;DR

Domingo Gomes is a California-based Spanish-language technology creator and founder of the media brand NewEsc. He runs a tech blog (newesc.com), a TikTok account with over 1.3 million followers, and presences on Instagram, YouTube, X, and Facebook. Self-described as the "Harry Potter de Tecnología," he covers app recommendations, browser shortcuts, Windows tips, smartphone reviews, AI tools, and gadget unboxings.

## Profile

Gomes founded newesc.com as a consumer technology blog and expanded it into a multi-platform media operation. The brand now produces short-form video content across TikTok, Instagram, and YouTube, alongside long-form written reviews and comparisons. Based in California, he has a strong Spanish-speaking audience and monetizes through affiliate links, merch (domingogomes.com), and sponsored hardware features.
