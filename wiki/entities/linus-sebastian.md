# Linus Sebastian

**Type:** Person / Content Creator

**Organization:** [[linus-media-group]]

## TL;DR

Linus Sebastian is the founder and host of Linus Tech Tips (LTT), one of the largest technology-focused YouTube channels. Through Linus Media Group, he runs a network of channels covering hardware reviews, PC builds, tech news, and a weekly podcast (WAN Show), with a combined subscriber base in the tens of millions.

## Profile

Linus started LTT in 2008 and has grown it into a dominant voice in consumer technology. The channel is known for:
- Deep-dive hardware reviews and benchmarks
- Educational PC building guides (including the canonical "How to build a PC")
- Consumer advocacy and investigative pieces
- Industry commentary and mainstream media collaborations (e.g., The Tonight Show with Jimmy Fallon)
