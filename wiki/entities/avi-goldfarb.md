# Avi Goldfarb

**Type:** Person / Economist / Academic  
**Institution:** Rotman School of Management, University of Toronto  
**Related:** [[ajay-agrawal]], [[joshua-gans]], [[prediction-machines]], [[power-and-prediction]]

## TL;DR

Avi Goldfarb is a professor and economist who co-authored *Prediction Machines* (2018) and *Power and Prediction* (2022) with Ajay Agrawal and Joshua Gans. Together, the trio popularized the "AI = prediction" framework at the intersection of economics, strategy, and technology.

## Key Works

- *Prediction Machines: The Simple Economics of Artificial Intelligence* (2018)
- *Power and Prediction: The Disruptive Economics of Artificial Intelligence* (2022)
