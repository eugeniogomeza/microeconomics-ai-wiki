# Anthropic

**Type:** Organization / AI Safety Company
**Also known as:** Creator of Claude
**URL:** [anthropic.com](https://www.anthropic.com/)
**Founded:** 2021

## TL;DR

Anthropic is an AI safety company founded by former OpenAI researchers. It develops the Claude family of LLMs and is known for its Constitutional AI approach to alignment. In addition to model development, Anthropic runs Anthropic Learn (training hub), contributes heavily to the Skills.sh ecosystem, and partners with enterprises through API and Claude Code.

## Profile

Anthropic positions itself at the intersection of frontier capability and safety. Its models (Claude 3/4 family, including Opus, Sonnet, and Haiku variants) compete directly with OpenAI's GPT series and Google's Gemini. Anthropic is a significant contributor to the broader agent ecosystem through:

- **Claude API** — Enterprise and developer access to Claude models
- **Claude Code** — Agentic IDE tool for software engineering
- **Model Context Protocol (MCP)** — Open standard for connecting LLMs to external tools
- **Anthropic Learn** — Training and certification platform for AI fluency
- **Skills.sh Contributions** — Top contributor by install volume (`frontend-design`, `skill-creator`, `pdf`, `pptx`, `xlsx`, `webapp-testing`, `mcp-builder`, `canvas-design`)
