# MIT Press

**Type:** Organization / Academic Publisher  
**URL:** [mitpress.mit.edu](https://mitpress.mit.edu/)  
**Related:** [[joshua-gans]], [[microeconomics-of-ai]]

## TL;DR

MIT Press is a leading academic publisher known for books in science, technology, and economics. They have increasingly adopted open-access models, including the "Direct to Open" initiative that made Joshua Gans's *The Microeconomics of Artificial Intelligence* freely available under a CC BY-NC-ND license.
