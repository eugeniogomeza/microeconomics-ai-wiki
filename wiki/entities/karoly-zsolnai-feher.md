# Károly Zsolnai-Fehér

**Type:** Person  
**Known For:** Creator of Two Minute Papers, computer graphics and AI researcher

## TL;DR

Károly Zsolnai-Fehér is the Hungarian-Austrian researcher and creator behind the YouTube channel Two Minute Papers. With a background in computer graphics and rendering, he built a channel that now reaches 1.7M+ subscribers by summarizing cutting-edge AI and CS research papers in two-minute episodes. His enthusiasm and visual comparisons have made research awareness accessible to a mass audience.

## Profile

Zsolnai-Fehér holds a PhD in computer graphics and has worked on rendering, simulation, and machine learning problems. Two Minute Papers started as a side project around 2014 and grew into a full-time content operation, producing 1,200+ episodes covering papers from NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR, SIGGRAPH, and other top venues.

The channel's value is speed of awareness: in 2 minutes, a viewer can understand a paper's problem, method, and result. The catchphrase at every video's end — a riff on "the possibilities are endless" — is a hallmark of the channel's optimism about technological progress.
