# Nous Research

## TL;DR

Nous Research is an open-source AI research collective that started as a Discord group of hackers training their own models. They have grown into a full research organization developing the Hermes model family and the Hermes agent harness. Their philosophy emphasizes getting out of the model's way and building lightweight, reliable infrastructure.

## Profile

Nous Research's origin story is a group of independent researchers on Discord experimenting with open-source model training. They later developed their own internal agent tool, which became Hermes after seeing OpenClaw's public release and realizing their version was superior.

## Key Products

- **Hermes LLM** — Open-source language model series.
- **Hermes Agent** — Open-source AI agent harness ([hermes-agent.nousresearch.com](https://hermes-agent.nousresearch.com/)).

## Philosophy

- **Get out of the model's way** — The model is the brain; the harness gives it hands and fingers.
- **Self-improvement** — Agents should grow better over time by crystallizing learnings into reusable skills.
- **Lightweight reliability** — Prefer fewer, well-integrated options over bloated marketplace ecosystems.
