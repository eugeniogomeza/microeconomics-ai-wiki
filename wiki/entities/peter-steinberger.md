# Peter Steinberger

**Type:** Person / Developer & Founder
**Also known as:** steipete
**Twitter/X:** [@steipete](https://x.com/steipete)

## TL;DR

Peter Steinberger is the creator of OpenClaw, an open-source personal AI assistant that gained viral traction in early 2026. In February 2026, Steinberger joined OpenAI while OpenClaw continues as an open-source project, an unusual arrangement that signals both personal-agent potential and major-platform validation.

## Profile

Steinberger built OpenClaw as a locally-hosted, self-improving AI assistant. The project attracted massive community enthusiasm, endorsements from high-profile figures (including Andrej Karpathy), and coverage in TechCrunch and The Verge. Despite joining OpenAI, the OpenClaw project remains open-source with sponsorship from OpenAI, GitHub, NVIDIA, Vercel, and others.
