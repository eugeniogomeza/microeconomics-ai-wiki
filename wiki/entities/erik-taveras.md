**Type:** Person

## TL;DR

Erik Taveras is a Dominican Republic-based backend engineer with 10+ years of experience and the founder of TalosFlow, a WhatsApp AI SaaS platform. He creates Spanish-language content on TikTok, YouTube, and other platforms about Python, n8n workflow automation, WhatsApp Business API chatbots, local LLMs, and AI agent deployment. He claims to have deployed 150+ systems and mentored 1,600+ engineers.

## Profile

Taveras built his career on backend engineering and business automation. His content is relentlessly practical: how to build chatbots, how to automate invoices via WhatsApp, how to run local LLMs with zero cloud cost. Unlike many AI educators who sell courses, he monetizes primarily through his TalosFlow SaaS and consulting bookings.
