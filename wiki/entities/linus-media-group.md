# Linus Media Group

**Type:** Organization / Media Company
**Also known as:** LMG
**Founder:** [[linus-sebastian]]

## TL;DR

Linus Media Group (LMG) is the production company behind Linus Tech Tips and its affiliated channels (ShortCircuit, Techquickie, TechLinked, GameLinked, Mac Address, The WAN Show). Based in Canada, LMG is one of the largest independent technology media organizations on YouTube.
