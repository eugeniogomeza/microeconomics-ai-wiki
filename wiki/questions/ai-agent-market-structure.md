# What Market Structure Will AI Agent Harnesses Converge To?

## Context

AI agent harnesses (Hermes, OpenClaw, etc.) sit between users and models. They provide memory, tools, and orchestration. The model layer (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) looks like a concentrated oligopoly. The harness layer is currently fragmented, with multiple open-source and commercial options competing.

## Current State of Knowledge

- OpenClaw was first-mover and had significant mindshare, but users report degradation and bloating over time.
- Hermes is gaining traction by being lighter, more stable, and philosophically opinionated ("get out of the model's way").
- LangChain and LangGraph are popular in the developer ecosystem but serve a different audience (application builders vs. personal agents).
- Most harnesses are free/open-source; monetization is unclear.

## Hypotheses

1. **Fragmentation persists** — Like Linux distributions, no single harness wins; users self-select by philosophy and workflow.
2. **One harness consolidates** — Network effects from skill marketplaces and integrations create a winner-take-most dynamic.
3. **Model providers absorb harnesses** — OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google build harness functionality directly into their API offerings, making standalone harnesses obsolete.
4. **Enterprise vs. personal split** — Different winners in enterprise (security/compliance) and personal (flexibility/vibe) markets.
