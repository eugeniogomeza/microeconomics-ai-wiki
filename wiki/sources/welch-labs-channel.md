## TL;DR

Welch Labs produces thoughtful, concept-first educational content on machine learning, AI, and mathematics. Best known for the **"Neural Networks Demystified"** series (2014–2015), which walks through building a complete neural network from scratch in Python. Stephen Welch is also writing "The Welch Labs Illustrated Guide to AI."

## Why It's Here

Welch Labs sits at the intersection of mathematical rigor and accessible presentation. The Neural Networks Demystified series is one of the earliest and still one of the best "build it from scratch" tutorials.

## Standout Content

### Neural Networks Demystified (2014–2015)
A 7-part series building and training a complete artificial neural network in Python:
1. **Data and Architecture** — Inputs, outputs, and network structure.
2. **Forward Propagation** — How data flows through the network.
3. **Gradient Descent** — Strategies for training.
4. **Backpropagation** — Computing gradients through the network.
5. **Numerical Gradient Checking** — Verifying backprop is correct.
6. **Training** — Putting it all together.
7. **Overfitting, Testing, and Regularization** — Real-world model validation.

**Supporting code:** [github.com/stephencwelch/Neural-Networks-Demystified](https://github.com/stephencwelch/Neural-Networks-Demystified)
