## TL;DR

Alan Daitch is a prominent Spanish-speaking AI and tech educator with a massive cross-platform presence. Former co-founder of Digodat (Google's #1 analytics partner in LATAM, sold to S4 Capital in 2020), former VP Data LATAM, Google Top Contributor, and LinkedIn Top Voice. He produces free courses and explainers on ChatGPT, Google Gemini, N8N automation, and AI content creation — explicitly refusing to sell courses. Also hosts the podcast "El Corazón de la Máquina."
