## TL;DR

A channel that summarizes cutting-edge AI and computer science research papers in roughly two minutes. Károly Zsolnai-Fehér reads the latest papers from venues like NeurIPS, ICML, CVPR, and SIGGRAPH, then creates succinct, visually engaging summaries that cut straight to the result. If you want to stay current on AI research without reading hundreds of papers, this is the fastest way.

## Why It's Here

Two Minute Papers fills a unique gap: research awareness at speed. While Karpathy teaches you to build systems and StatQuest teaches you the math, Two Minute Papers keeps you informed about what's happening at the frontier — new architectures, training techniques, generative models, and scientific breakthroughs.

## Format and Style

- **Length:** ~2 minutes per paper (some special episodes go longer).
- **Structure:** Problem → Method → Result → Implication. No math, no code.
- **Visuals:** Side-by-side comparisons (before/after), generated outputs, animations of results.
- **Tone:** Enthusiastic and accessible. Every video ends with the catchphrase about the "possibilities" of the result.

## Standout Breakthrough Summaries

### Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF)
- Summarized the 2020 Mildenhall et al. paper that changed 3D scene reconstruction.
- Key idea: represent a scene as a continuous 5D neural radiance field rather than discrete voxels or meshes.
- Enabled photorealistic novel view synthesis from just a few images.

### Stable Diffusion / DALL-E / Text-to-Image
- Covered every major text-to-image milestone (DALL-E 2, Stable Diffusion, Midjourney, Imagen).
- Showed how diffusion models progressively denoise random noise into images conditioned on text prompts.
- Demonstrated the speed at which generative AI visual capabilities improved (2020–2024).

### AlphaFold / AlphaFold 2
- Covered DeepMind's protein folding breakthroughs.
- Explained how attention-based architectures predicted 3D protein structures from amino acid sequences.
- Highlighted the biological significance: solving a 50-year grand challenge in structural biology.

### GPT-3 / GPT-4 / Large Language Models
- Summarized papers on scaling laws, emergent abilities, and the "stochastic parrot" debate.
- Covered Chain-of-Thought reasoning and in-context learning papers.
- Showed how prompting strategies unlocked capabilities not present during standard training.

### Realistic Neural Talking Heads
- Deepfake and digital human synthesis papers.
- Single-image face animation, neural avatars, and video portrait synthesis.
