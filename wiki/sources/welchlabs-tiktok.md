## Summary

Welch Labs' TikTok is the short-form extension of Stephen Welch's acclaimed YouTube channel. Where YouTube hosts deep-dive animated series like *Neural Networks Demystified* and *Imaginary Numbers are Real*, TikTok delivers rapid, beautifully animated explainers on AI history, math discoveries, science stories, and breakthrough moments.
