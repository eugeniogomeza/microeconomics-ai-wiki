## TL;DR

The definitive practitioner guide to building production AI applications with foundation models. Covers the full lifecycle from model selection and prompt engineering to evaluation, RAG, fine-tuning, deployment, and observability. Written by a former Google Brain engineer and current AI startup executive.

## Key Claims

1. **AI is now a systems engineering problem** — Foundation models have made building AI apps accessible, but the challenge has shifted from training models to orchestrating them reliably at scale.
2. **Model-as-a-service changes the game** — The ability to swap models dynamically means evaluation and robustness become more important than model selection.
3. **Prompt engineering is a first-class engineering practice** — It's not just trial-and-error; it's a systematic discipline with patterns, templates, and optimization techniques.
4. **RAG is the default architecture for knowledge-intensive apps** — Most production AI apps don't need fine-tuning; they need better retrieval.
5. **Evaluation is the hardest part** — Deterministic software engineering gives way to probabilistic systems where traditional unit tests don't apply.
