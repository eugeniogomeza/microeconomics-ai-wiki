## TL;DR

NewEsc is a Spanish-language technology media brand founded by Domingo Gomes. It spans a consumer tech blog (newesc.com), a massive TikTok presence (@newesc, ~1.3M followers), Instagram, YouTube, and a merch shop. Content covers tech tips, app recommendations ("Super Apps"), browser shortcuts, Windows/PC tutorials, smartphone unboxings, AI tools, drones, and software deals. Self-described as the "Harry Potter de Tecnología."

## Why It's Here

NewEsc is a major Spanish-language tech education presence with a multi-platform ecosystem. Their TikTok shorts-style explainers make tech and AI tools accessible to a large Spanish-speaking audience. As a media brand with multiple contributors, it is structurally more complex than a typical solo creator channel.

## Content Themes

- **Super Apps** — App recommendations and productivity tools
- **Web Útiles** — Browser shortcuts, Chrome tricks, web tools
- **iPhone / Android** — Smartphone tips, unboxings, reviews
- **Windows / Office** — PC tutorials, license deal tips
- **AI Tools** — Showcasing aggregator platforms and individual AI apps
- **Drones / Gadgets** — Hardware unboxings (e.g., CMF Phone 2 Pro, HOVERAir X1 PROMAX)

## Background

Domingo Gomes is based in California, USA, and founded newesc.com as a consumer tech blog. The brand has expanded into short-form video with a team of writers and a large social following. Monetization includes affiliate links (e.g., GVMall), merch (domingogomes.com), and sponsored hardware features.
