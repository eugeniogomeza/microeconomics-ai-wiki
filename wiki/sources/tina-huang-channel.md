## TL;DR

Ex-Meta data scientist turned full-time content creator. Tina Huang makes practical AI, coding, data science, and tech career content — most notably in short-form YouTube Shorts. Her philosophy: "minimize effort and maximize outcome." She covers how people actually use AI in their jobs, not how demos say it works.

## Why It's Here

Tina Huang fills a gap between high-level AI hype and on-the-ground reality. As a former Meta data scientist, she brings credibility to career advice: how to self-learn data science, how to transition into AI roles, how to use AI tools productively, and how to navigate tech careers without burning out.

## Standout Content

### AI and Data Science Career Advice
- How people actually use AI in their jobs vs. how demos portray it.
- Self-learning roadmaps for data science and machine learning.
- Freelancing and content creation as alternative tech careers.

### YouTube Shorts
- Quick, dense explainers on Python, SQL, data tools, and AI trends.
- Productivity and "lazy" approaches to learning technical topics.

### Interviews and Long-Form
- Podcast appearances discussing the transition from corporate data scientist to creator.
- Honest takes on the AI job market, bootcamps, and degree requirements.

## Philosophy

> "My goal is always to minimize effort and maximise outcome. I basically refuse to do things the hard way."

Huang advocates for learning through doing, using AI tools to augment productivity, and building publicly rather than waiting until you're "expert enough."
