## TL;DR

The single best mathematics visualization channel on YouTube. Grant Sanderson uses custom-built animation tools to explain complex topics in math, physics, CS, and most relevantly for this wiki: neural networks and transformers. If you want to *see* how attention works or how a neural net learns, this is the canonical reference.

## Why It's Here

3Blue1Brown fills a unique niche: making the *mechanics* of AI visually intuitive. Where Krish Naik teaches you to *build* models, 3Blue1Brown teaches you to *understand* them at a geometric level.

## Philosophy

> "The goal is to use animation to help viewers develop genuine intuitions for mathematical ideas."

Sanderson's approach is not tutorial or code-along. It's concept-first, visualization-heavy, and targets the "aha moment" where abstract math becomes concrete geometry.
