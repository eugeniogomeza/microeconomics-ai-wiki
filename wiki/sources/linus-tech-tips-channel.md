# Linus Tech Tips — YouTube Channel

**Author:** [[linus-sebastian]]

## TL;DR

Linus Tech Tips (LTT) is one of the largest technology-focused YouTube channels, hosted by Linus Sebastian. Content spans consumer hardware reviews, PC builds, technology news, and commentary, with affiliated channels covering shorts (ShortCircuit), quick explainers (Techquickie), tech news (TechLinked), gaming (GameLinked), Apple (Mac Address), and the weekly WAN Show podcast.

## Channel Stats & Structure

- **Main channel:** Linus Tech Tips (@LinusTechTips)
