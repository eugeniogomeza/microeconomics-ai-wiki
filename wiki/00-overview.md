## Current Focus

Building a curated knowledge base for understanding AI through three lenses: economic theory, agent engineering, and statistical methods.

## Thesis / North Star

AI is most productively understood as (1) a prediction technology with microeconomic implications, (2) an agent infrastructure problem where memory and tools matter more than raw model capability, and (3) a domain where simulation and empirical validation outperform theoretical guesswork.
